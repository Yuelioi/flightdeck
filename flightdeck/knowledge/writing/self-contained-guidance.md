# Make reusable guidance self-contained

Knowledge should remain understandable without reopening the Work that produced it. State the
durable conclusion, the context needed to apply it, and a concise example or procedure when useful.

Source links may support archaeology, but they should not carry the only explanation. Progress
notes, failed attempts, and temporary decisions belong in the Work; promote them only after they can
be rewritten as positive guidance useful beyond that Work.
