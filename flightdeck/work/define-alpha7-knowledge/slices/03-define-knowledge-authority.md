# Define Knowledge's authority during Work

## Outcome

A Work knows whether selected Knowledge advises judgment, mandates behavior, or supplies evidence,
and where genuinely enforceable rules belong.

## Current

Knowledge advises AI judgment and execution but neither mandates behavior nor proves compliance.
A Work may depart from relevant guidance when its concrete situation warrants. Rules that cannot
be violated belong in tests, validators, or repository instructions.
