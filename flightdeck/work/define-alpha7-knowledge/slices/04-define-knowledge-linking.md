# Define when Work links Knowledge

## Outcome

Selected Knowledge becomes durable Work state only when a fresh session needs the relationship,
without creating consultation logs, copied guidance, or an ever-growing dependency list.

## Current

Selected Knowledge becomes durable Work state only when the relationship has continuing recovery
value. Immediately required guidance may occupy one of Next's three links; continuing support may
appear under References. Ordinary consultation is not logged, and guidance is not copied into Work.
